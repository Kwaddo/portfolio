Ali Khalaf. Software Engineer. Bahrain. Go, TypeScript, React, Laravel, Rust. English and Arabic.

## Chapter 1: Where it started

I'm Ali, a software engineer from Bahrain. I finished school in 2021 and started a Software Engineering degree at the University of Bahrain the same year.

The first thing I shipped for fun was a game. S.M.O.B.B., a text adventure about AI and the people around it, had its first commit in July 2023, and I kept returning to it for three years.

## Chapter 2: Learning by building

In 2024 I joined Reboot Coding Institute alongside university. It pushed me into Go, and into the habit of building real things to learn: a CHIP-8 emulator, a raycasting engine, an ant-pathing solver, a maze game with a multiplayer mode, and a forum.

Working in teams there taught me the part I enjoy most, which is connecting frontend and backend so a project holds together.

## Chapter 3: Games on the side

Games are where I experiment. S.M.O.B.B. grew into a released Rust game with a terminal version and a web build. skoobl is a free multiplayer drawing-and-guessing party game you can play in a browser, with English and Arabic word packs. smoke-break is a maze game where you hunt for a friend on a smoke break.

## Chapter 4: Working in the real world

My first jobs were short and fast. In 2025 I interned at DOO Mena, building a backend system for one of their sections, and joined Croozz Technologies, a small team shipping updates as quickly as we could. At Tech Bay IT Solutions I trained on Dynamics 365 and worked with clients, many of them government bodies. At Babel Pastries I built a TV slideshow application with an admin panel, deployed it, and trained a new developer on Laravel and React.

In 2026 I graduated from both Reboot (with DevOps) and the University of Bahrain, and joined Tanami as a Software Engineer.
