It was the middle of winter and we were looking for our mentor from Reboot, who had disappeared. We would usually find him in his smoke spots, but he was not there at all.

On our way back, going up the stairs, Yusuf looked as if a lightbulb had appeared over his head. "What if we made a game about this?" he said. That was exactly what we did. It was a vital Reboot project, so we started planning.
