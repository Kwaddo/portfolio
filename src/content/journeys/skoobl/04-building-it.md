We split the work. I took the game itself and Moataz designed it as he saw fit.

The backend is in Go, a given choice since I planned to host it on a $6 server and wanted something that would work well for what I assumed was a temporary showcase. The frontend is React with TanStack Router.

Moataz designed it extremely well. When the game was done I had a bunch of friends play it, and they had fun. On a roll, we got to work on two more game modes.
