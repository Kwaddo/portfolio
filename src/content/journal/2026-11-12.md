Project Akalaat is still underway, I am yet to have a meeting with the design lead regarding future design inclusions and simply putting everything on the table for both sides. For all I know, I might make an eventual migration to Steam so that a free download to the client can be more comforting than a website. This is so we stick to a faster protocol than making use of WebRTC despite it being more than feasible.

For what was meant to just be a challenge ended up being far more exciting to be further involved in.
